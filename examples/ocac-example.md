# OCAC Example: Sip & Social, rewritten with the Whimsy Kit

This shows how the brand kit (`brand/brand-kit.md`) applies to a real past post. Every
fact comes from the original caption. Nothing was invented except the 18+ line, which
is club-wide policy.

**What changed vs. the original:** a plain-text searchable title inside the 🫧 frame,
⟡ section headers, the fixed details block with the age line, a newcomer-friendly send
CTA, a Discord sign-off, the signature closer, and hashtags. The original had none.
