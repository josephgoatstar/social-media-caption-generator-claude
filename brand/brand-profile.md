## Identity
- **Handles:** Instagram @ocanimeclub · TikTok @ocanimeclub · Discord discord.gg/ocanimeclub
- **Short name:** OC Anime Club (OCAC in casual/Discord copy)
- **What we are:** an 18+ social club for anime fans in Southern California.
  **Community first, event host second.** People come to make friends and connect.
- **What we post:** our events (cosplay meetups, socials, speed friending, D&D,
  convention meetups, raves, virtual game nights and watch parties, volleyball,
  markets) and general posts promoting the club and community.
- **Who it's for:** anime fans aged 18–30 in SoCal (OC, LA, IE) who want friends, game,
  watch anime, and cosplay.
- **What we want them to do (in order):** follow and stick around → engage (send, save,
  comment) → come to events → **join the Discord**.
- **The Discord is the hub:** it's where friendships actually happen, and members
  **hear about events and hangouts first**. Every public post should give a reason to join.

## Voice
- **Brand mission line:** "keeping SoCal whimsical" · "spreading whimsy one friend at a time"
- **3 words:** whimsical, welcoming, straight-to-the-point
- **We sound like:** the friend who's always planning the next hangout and drags you
  along. Cute and excited, but the details are always clear.
- **Values:** self-expression, being yourself, making friends, everyone belongs.
- **Emoji use:** heavy. Most paragraphs, headers, or every other sentence get one.
  Use the brand palette in `brand-kit.md`, not random emoji.
- **Style moves we use:** cutesy slang, kaomoji, cybersigilism-style glyphs,
  anime quotes and copypasta references, meme lines, exclamations.
- **Guardrail:** whimsy goes on top of the info and never replaces it. Event name, date,
  time, place, price, and age are always plain and easy to scan.
- **Capitalization:** mostly lowercase in body copy. EVENT NAMES may be ALL CAPS
  (our top post led with the name in caps). Venue names keep proper casing.
- **Never:** corporate tone ("We are pleased to announce"), guilt-trippy FOMO, mean or
  gatekeep-y humor ("real fans only"), or anything that makes newcomers feel like outsiders.

## What's working (from our top posts)
- **KH Halloween Town Rave, first announcement (2,200 likes / 1,807 shares):**
  - The event name in CAPS was line 1, followed by a genre line with ✦ separators.
  - It had a lineup block, a finale callout, plain logistics, scarcity that was true
    ("ALMOST SOLD OUT"), a feature list, and a clear 21+/security line.
  - It ended with a fandom quote in blackletter ("darkness becomes light…") that
    fans recognized.
  - **Missing:** an explicit share CTA and a Discord mention.
- **Sip & Social speed friending (297 likes / 177 shares):**
  - The event name was framed by 🫧, and the hook said why it exists ("designed
    to actually make making friends easy").
  - ✦ bullets, "capped at 70" (specific and personal), a short details block.
- **Takeaway:** shares are about 60–80% of likes on our event posts. People DM them
  to friends to plan. Every event caption should hand them the message to send.
  Comments are low, so add an easy question when engagement is the goal.
