# Instagram

**Signal hierarchy:** DM shares (sends) > saves > meaningful comments > watch time /
completion > likes. Sends are the strongest reach signal for reaching non-followers;
saves are the strongest for carousels. Instagram search reads captions, on-screen
text, alt text, and audio — write captions people could search for.

Hard limits: caption 2,200 chars · hashtags capped at 5 · ~125 chars visible in feed
before "… more" (Reels show roughly 1–2 lines over the video).

---

## Reels
- **Caption length:** 80–200 chars. The video carries the content; the caption frames it.
- **On-screen hook (first 1–2 s):** ≤ 7 words, high contrast, placed inside the safe zone
  (not bottom 20% where caption/UI covers it). This is the real hook — the caption is backup.
- **Structure:** hook line → 1–2 lines of context/payoff tease → CTA → 3–5 hashtags.
- **Best CTAs:** "Send this to the friend who…" (sends) · "Save this for your next…" (saves) ·
  a specific question (comments).
- **Keyword:** primary search phrase in the first sentence ("NYC rooftop bars",
  "beginner leg day").
- **Don't:** start with an emoji, "Hey guys", or restate the on-screen text word-for-word.

## Feed (single image / flyer)
- **Length:** 125–600 chars. The first 125 chars must work alone.
- Image should say the *what*; caption adds the *why*, story, or detail the image can't.
- For flyers/events: caption = hook + 1 line of value + logistics recap (date · time ·
  place · price) + CTA. See `formats.md` → Flyers.
- Always write **alt text** (≤ 125 chars, describes image + keyword). Helps search + access.

## Carousel
- Up to 20 slides. **Slide 1 = the hook headline** (≤ 8 words, promise a specific payoff).
  Slide 2 must pay off immediately or people swipe away. Last slide = CTA (save/share/follow).
- **Caption length:** 300–900 chars. People who stop on carousels read.
- **Structure:** hook → 2–4 lines that add context the slides don't (why it matters, the
  story, a bonus tip) → save CTA → hashtags.
- CTA default: save ("Save this so you have it when…"). Second choice: share.
- Use numbered or emoji-bulleted lines with line breaks — never a wall of text.

## Stories
Stories have **no caption** — you're writing on-screen text and choosing stickers.
- 1 idea per frame, ≤ 12 words of text per frame, readable in ~2 s. 3–7 frames per sequence.
- Frame 1 = hook ("Real talk about…", "Big news 👀", "You voted, here's the result").
- Use an interactive sticker on at least one frame — polls, quizzes, question box,
  emoji slider, countdown (for events/drops), "Add Yours". Replies land in DMs, which is
  a strong closeness signal for future reach.
- **Link sticker** goes on its own frame with a reason to tap ("Tap for the full guide").
- Keep text out of the top ~14% and bottom ~20% (profile bar / reply bar).
- Countdown sticker for any event or drop — followers get reminded automatically.

## Collab posts / Trial Reels
- Collab: write the caption so it makes sense to BOTH audiences; tag the partner in line 1–2.
- Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first): write for strangers — no inside jokes, explicit hook.

## Hashtags
- 3–5, niche and specific to this post. Mix: 1–2 niche community + 1–2 topic + 1 branded/local.
- Rotate — never the identical set every post.
- Never: #fyp #explore #viral #instagood #like4like #followforfollow #reels (as a sole tag).
