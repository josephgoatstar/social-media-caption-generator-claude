# Anti-Patterns — Never Write These

## Engagement bait (platforms demote it)
- "Like if you agree" · "Comment YES" · "Tag 3 friends" · "Share before they delete this"
- "React with [emoji] if…" on Instagram/Facebook/TikTok
  (reaction-to-RSVP on **Discord** is fine — it's a functional poll, not bait)
- "Follow for follow", "Double tap"

## Hashtag crimes
- #fyp #foryou #foryoupage #viral #trending #explore #explorepage #instagood
  #like4like #follow4follow #followforfollow #xyzbca #reels (as sole tag) #shorts (as sole tag)
- More than 5 hashtags · the same set on every post · hashtags unrelated to the post

## Filler openers (kill the hook)
- "Hey guys" · "In today's video" · "So…" · "Let's dive in" · "Check this out"
- "I'm so excited to announce" (say the news instead) · "Happy [weekday]!"

## Hype words that read as AI/ad copy (use only if in the brand voice)
- game-changer, revolutionary, unlock your potential, elevate, delve, seamless,
  next level, in today's fast-paced world, look no further, we've got you covered

## Risky claims / moderation triggers
- Guaranteed income, get-rich-quick, "100% guaranteed", miracle results
- Health or weight-loss promises; before/after body claims
- Violence words even as slang in public captions ("killing it" is fine; threats are not)
- Character swaps (f*ck, s3x, $ex) don't evade filters — rephrase instead
- Fake urgency/scarcity ("only 3 left!") unless true

## Format crimes
- Walls of text — max ~2 lines per paragraph on mobile
- Emoji as the first character of a Reel caption, or emoji replacing keywords
- Same caption pasted across platforms
- Raw long URLs in captions (IG doesn't link them; use "link in bio"/masked links on Discord)
- @everyone for routine Discord posts; pings at the top of the message
- Inventing stats, testimonials, prices, or dates
