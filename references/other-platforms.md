# Threads, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, X

## Threads
- **Signal:** replies (and reply-chains) above all.
- **Length:** 100–280 chars optimal, 500 max.
- One idea, conversational, like texting a group chat. Opinions and questions win.
- **1 topic tag** only (can be multi-word, niche > broad).
- Don't cross-post IG captions with hashtags — reads as spam.

## Facebook (Page / Personal)
- **Length:** 40–80 chars for text-led posts; video posts can be a bit longer.
- Ultra-short hook → value → a real discussion question.
- 0–2 hashtags or none.
- **Avoid reach-killing salesy words:** "buy now", "limited time", "FREE" (caps),
  "click here", "giveaway", "contest", "make money", "income".
  **Safer swaps:** "available today", "complimentary" / "no cost", "learn more",
  "we're picking a winner", "for a chance to win".
- Links in the post body reduce reach — say "link in comments" and post the link as the first comment.

## Facebook Group
- **Length:** 200–500 chars, contextual and personal.
- Community phrasing: "Curious how you all handle…", "Who else has…".
- End with an open question. No links in the body.

## YouTube Shorts
- **Title:** 40–70 chars (100 max). The #1 search signal — keyword first, then benefit
  or curiosity gap. Must match the video exactly (mismatch tanks retention).
  Formulas: "[Keyword]: [what you get]" · "How to [result] in [time]" ·
  "[N] [topic] mistakes" · "Why [common belief] is wrong".
- **Description:** 150–300 chars, first 100 visible. Hook → 1–2 value lines → CTA → 3–5 hashtags at end.
- CTA: subscribe or "watch the full video" (related video link) — channel growth > likes.
- Never #shorts as the only tag.

## X (Twitter)
- **Length:** ≤ 280 chars (non-premium). Best: 70–200.
- Hook is the whole post. Concrete claim, number, or contrarian take.
- 0–1 hashtags. Links reduce reach — put link in a reply.
- For longer content: a thread with a 1/ hook post that stands alone.
