# TikTok

**Signal hierarchy:** completion rate / rewatches > shares > comments > saves > likes.
TikTok is a search engine for younger users: it reads **spoken words > on-screen text >
caption > hashtags**. Say the keyword out loud and put it on screen in the first seconds.

Hard limits: caption up to 4,000 chars · ~first line visible (≈80–100 chars) before
"more" · photo-mode posts have a separate **title** field (keep ≤ 90 chars).

---

## Video
- **Deliver 3 hook layers** — they must agree, not repeat word-for-word:
  1. **Spoken hook** (first sentence of the voiceover, ≤ 2 s)
  2. **On-screen text hook** (≤ 7 words, appears frame 1)
  3. **Caption line 1** (≤ 80 chars, includes the search keyword)
- **Caption length:** 100–300 chars for most posts. Go long (500–2,000) only for
  search-intent "how to" content where the caption works as a mini-article.
- **Structure:** keyword-rich hook → 1–2 lines of tease ("#3 is the one nobody uses") →
  CTA → 3–5 hashtags.
- **Completion levers to tease in caption:** a numbered payoff ("the last one…"), a reveal,
  a before/after, a result at the end.
- **CTA:** a specific question gets comments ("Which one are you trying first — 1 or 3?");
  "Send this to…" gets shares; "Part 2?" only if you'll actually post it.
- Emoji: 1–3, at line ends, never replacing keywords.

## Photo mode (carousel)
- **Title** (≤ 90 chars): the keyword headline.
- **Slide 1 text:** the hook, big and short. Last slide: CTA or "save for later."
- **Description:** 150–500 chars, list-style, keyword-rich.

## TikTok Stories / Now
- Casual, 1 line of text, behind-the-scenes. Use for reminders ("live in 1 hr") not main content.

## LIVE / event promos
- Caption names the exact time with time zone ("LIVE tonight 8pm ET") and the reason to show up.

## Hashtags
- 3–5 MAX. Formula: 1 niche community + 1 topic/keyword + 1 trending (only if it's
  genuinely relevant) + optional branded.
- Never: #fyp #foryou #foryoupage #viral #xyzbca #trending.

## TikTok voice notes
- Lowercase-friendly, conversational, a little self-aware. Sounds like a person, not an ad.
- Avoid ad-speak ("Shop now!", "Limited offer!") — use "link's in bio if you want it".
